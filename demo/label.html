Vestigium · a benchmark on real records

Is the seller the previous buyer?

Each item is one link in a real Philadelphia property chain: an earlier deed and the next deed for the same property. You see only the register entries, never what the engine decided. Your answers become the answer key. They are saved on this computer only.

How to judge
  • Judge the names only. Ignore the document type (a sheriff's deed lists the foreclosed owner as the seller).
  • Same party: the later seller is clearly the earlier buyer (names in "SURNAME FIRSTNAME" order; a middle initial or an extra co-owner does not matter; if several names are listed, it is enough that one clearly matches).
  • Same party, written differently: a misspelling or variant of the same person or company ("KRZSYTOF"/"KRZYSZTOF", "HOLDING"/"HOLDINGS"), or that person's estate, executor or trustee.
  • Different party: someone else sells: a relative, a different company, or a name with no connection.
  • Can't tell: the records genuinely do not let you decide. Use sparingly.
